**Course Description**: Accelerating advances in AI, including Large Language Models (LLMs), Deep Reinforcement Learning (DRL), and Physics Inspired Neural Networks (PINNs) are collectively revolutionizing design automation. The state of practice in design automation for Cyber Physical Systems is Model based Systems Engineering (MBSE), where design are manually constructed and represented in myriads of domain-specific modeling languages & tools (Simulink, Modelica, UML, SysML, CAD); designs are evaluated in foundational analysis methods (discrete event simulations, differential algebraic equation solvers, finite element methods); and designs are optimized through multi disciplinary analysis and optimization methods. This state of practices is rapidly being transformed with new AI based methods. Generative design techniques and LLMs are being applied for design construction, DRL is being used for design exploration and optimization, while PINNs are being used for accelerating design analysis and evaluation. In this seminar course we will conduct a thorough review of model-based design automation, explore AI-based advances in design automation, and identify opportunities for impactful research and development in this rapidly advancing field.
